# B8 Classical Machine Learning & Data Science

## Mission

Развить способность корректно формулировать ML-задачи, строить воспроизводимые модели и оценивать их реальную полезность без leakage и самообмана.

## In scope

Problem framing, data/statistics, feature engineering, classical models, scikit-learn, validation/metrics, interpretability и experimentation.

## Non-goals

Distributed data pipelines — B4; deep learning — B9; LLM applications — B10; model lifecycle/platform — B11.

## Clusters

- `b8.cluster.problem-framing` — target, decision, baseline и business/technical metric.
- `b8.cluster.data-understanding-quality` — sampling, missingness, bias и data quality.
- `b8.cluster.statistics-inference` — uncertainty, estimation, tests и causal caution.
- `b8.cluster.features-preprocessing` — encoding, scaling, selection и leakage-safe transforms.
- `b8.cluster.supervised-learning` — regression, classification, ensembles и calibration.
- `b8.cluster.unsupervised-learning` — clustering, dimensionality reduction и anomaly detection.
- `b8.cluster.evaluation-validation` — splits, cross-validation, metrics, leakage и robustness.
- `b8.cluster.sklearn-pipelines` — estimators, pipelines, tuning и reproducibility.
- `b8.cluster.interpretability-responsibility` — explanations, fairness, limitations и human oversight.
- `b8.cluster.experiments-reproducibility` — experiment design, baselines, tracking boundaries и reporting.

## Hard baseline prerequisites

A1 probability/statistics, A2 и B1.

## Conditional prerequisites

Нет.

## Strong connections

B4, B9, B10, B11, C1.
