# C1 Engineering Leadership & Architecture

## Mission

Развить способность формировать техническое направление, согласовывать решения между командами и снижать системные/организационные риски без превращения себя в bottleneck.

## In scope

Problem framing, architecture trade-offs, RFC/ADR, strategy, migrations, governance, economics, incident leadership, influence, mentoring и organizational learning.

## Non-goals

C1 не заменяет техническую глубину B-треков, people management/HR discipline и полноценный курс бизнес-стратегии или коммуникационной лингвистики.

## Clusters

- `c1.cluster.problem-framing-constraints` — цели, stakeholders, requirements, assumptions и constraints.
- `c1.cluster.quality-attributes-tradeoffs` — quality attributes, alternatives и reversibility.
- `c1.cluster.decomposition-ownership` — boundaries, ownership, interfaces и socio-technical design.
- `c1.cluster.architecture-communication` — RFC, ADR, diagrams, review и decision records.
- `c1.cluster.strategy-roadmap` — technical vision, sequencing, options и portfolio thinking.
- `c1.cluster.evolution-migrations` — evolutionary architecture, legacy modernization и migrations.
- `c1.cluster.risk-security-compliance` — systemic risk, security, privacy и regulatory constraints.
- `c1.cluster.reliability-incident-leadership` — SLO strategy, crisis leadership и systemic learning.
- `c1.cluster.platform-standards-dx` — standards, golden paths, governance и developer experience.
- `c1.cluster.engineering-economics` — build/buy, TCO, opportunity cost и vendor risk.
- `c1.cluster.influence-mentoring-succession` — influence, delegation, mentoring и succession.
- `c1.cluster.feedback-organizational-learning` — metrics, feedback loops, postmortems и adaptation.

## Hard baseline prerequisites

Нет формального gate: C1 развивается параллельно.

- Outcomes L4–L5 требуют evidence из 2–4 depth-треков и межкомандных задач.
